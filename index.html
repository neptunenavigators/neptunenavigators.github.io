What We Do

We develop cutting-edge AI systems that automate infrastructure inspection using autonomous drones and deep learning. Our flagship product, HARRIS (High-precision Autonomous Railway & Rail-track Inspection System), revolutionizes how ports and railways maintain their critical infrastructure.

🚁 Autonomous UAV Inspection

Enterprise-grade drones with RGB, thermal imaging, and RTK positioning

🧠 AI-Powered Detection

Deep learning models detect defects, obstacles, and anomalies in real-time

🗺️ Digital Twin Technology

3D reconstruction and interactive mapping of entire infrastructure networks

HARRIS System

Our autonomous inspection platform combines computer vision, thermal analysis, and 3D mapping to detect railway track anomalies before they cause operational disruptions.

  • 60% cost reduction compared to manual inspection
  • 95% faster defect detection
  • 24/7 monitoring capability
  • Predictive maintenance through historical data analysis

Currently piloting with Port Gdynia for railway and crane track inspection.
